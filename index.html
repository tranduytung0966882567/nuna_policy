1. Introduction

Welcome to Nuna: Photo & Video ("Nuna", "the App", "we", "our", or "us").

Nuna is a photo and video editing application that provides creative tools for editing photos and videos, including filters, effects, frames, adjustments, and other editing features.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use Nuna.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Photos and Videos

Nuna may request access to photos and videos on your device when you choose to import media for editing.

The photos and videos you select are used to provide the editing features of the App, such as filters, effects, frames, adjustments, and video editing.

We do not use your personal photos or videos for advertising.

Unless a feature specifically requires otherwise, media selected for editing is processed on your device and is not automatically uploaded to our servers.

2.2 Analytics Information

Nuna uses Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve our products and services.

Depending on the configuration of the App and your device, analytics information may include:

  • App events and interactions
  • Features used
  • Session information
  • App version
  • Operating system
  • Device information
  • General usage information

2.3 Crash Information

Nuna uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify, diagnose, and resolve crashes and technical problems.

Crash information may include crash logs, device information, operating system information, application version, and technical information associated with the state of the App when a crash occurs.

2.4 Performance Information

Nuna uses Firebase Performance Monitoring to monitor and improve application performance.

This may include technical information relating to app startup time, network requests, screen rendering, and other performance metrics.
